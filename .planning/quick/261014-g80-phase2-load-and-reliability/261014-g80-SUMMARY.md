## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - Bug] `synced_through` formula.** The plan's "min over days of (day close if final else synced_at)" made a fully synced month report its first day's close (seen live: `2026-06-01T23:00:00Z` for all of June). Changed to the oldest `synced_at` among non-final days, or the window end when every day is final, still clamped to min(end, now). Commit e721332, test updated.
2. **Availability cycle ok rule.** A cycle is `ok` unless the breaker stopped it or at least half of the calls failed, so one persistent 404 does not trigger a 56-call retry every 30 minutes.
3. **Forced first availability cycle.** To produce the status file without waiting about 2 h, I backdated the `availability` `job_state.last_run` by 30,000 s on the RPi; the loop picked it up on its next 5-min check (56/56 refreshed).
4. **STATE.md row skipped** as instructed (the orchestrator adds it). SUMMARY.md and STATE.md are not committed by me.
